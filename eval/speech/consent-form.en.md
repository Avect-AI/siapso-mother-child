# Consent form: voice recordings of medicine and remedy names

Template for adult volunteers. Complete the placeholders before use. No patient is asked to take part, and nothing about anyone's health is recorded.

## Project

SIAPSO Mother-Child is a free, open-source app that helps check medicines and traditional remedies during pregnancy and breastfeeding. It can listen to the name of a product spoken in French or Wolof. To measure how well it understands, the team needs recordings of people saying those names.

Contact for questions: `<to be completed by the team>`

## What you are asked to do

Read aloud a short list of names of medicines and traditional remedies (for example "paracetamol" or "kinkeliba"), one at a time, in French, in Wolof, or both. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

## What is recorded

- Only your voice saying these names. You say nothing about your health, your family or your life.
- Your recording is linked to a code (for example `spk-007`), never to your name. Your name and signature stay on this form, which the team keeps separately and never publishes.

## How the recordings are used and published

- To measure the accuracy of the open speech model used by the app, and to improve how the app handles names.
- The recordings, with their text and your code, will be published openly under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0). Anyone may then copy and reuse them, including for commercial purposes, with credit to the project. A voice can sometimes identify a person, even without a name.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary. You may refuse, stop at any moment, or skip any name.
- You may withdraw later by contacting the team at the address above, quoting your code or the date of recording. The team will remove your recordings from the project and from future releases. Copies already published and downloaded by others cannot be recalled.
- Refusing or withdrawing has no consequence for you.
