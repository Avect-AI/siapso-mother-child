# Prescription reading evaluation

`pnpm eval:prescription` runs the reading of the app (`app/src/domain/prescription-reading.ts`: text lines in, products of the base out) on the text that OCR engines gave for 36 synthetic prescriptions, and on their exact text, and scores it against what each prescription says. It fails CI on a false product above the baseline, on a recall below the baseline, and on a combination read in part without its warning. Design and measures: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-prescription-scan-design.md` (SCRUM-57).

No image is read here and none is committed: only text. The prescriptions are synthetic (generated by `tools/prescription-eval/`, synthetic names, no real patient, no real prescription).

The OCR texts were made on 2026-11-05 by the spike of the spec (section 4), with Chromium 156, from the images of `node tools/prescription-eval/generate.mjs <out-dir> 36 <fonts-dir>`; the images were never committed. The source of each text is the `id` of its prescription and the `variant` of the image. The exact text (`text` source) is `truthText` of `synth.mjs`, computed at run time. The texts are as the engines gave them, but for one change: the long dashes Tesseract read in rules and strokes are written as en dashes (U+2013), since the repository holds no em dash; the reading treats both alike, and the figures are the same.

## What is measured

For each source, by set (`dev`, `heldout`), by image (`scan`, `photo`, `hard`, `dim`, `pdf-text`, `pdf-scan`) and by writing (`print`, `hand`, the handwriting-style proxy):

- **recall**: the products of the pack found, among those on the prescriptions; **precision**: the right products among those proposed. A product is right when its substances are those of a product of the prescription.
- **false products**: proposals whose substances are those of no product of the prescription, with those that are a part of a combination of the prescription ("part") and how many of them carry the warning that the line names more ("warned"), and those read on the line of a product outside the pack ("out").
- **strengths**: the strength read on the line equals the one written, or differs ("wrong"); none read is neither.
- **units read**: the units per day the posology reader gives for every product found; **offered**: what the list offers, for the products a daily-maximum rule watches only (`needsUnitsPerDay`), right or wrong. A posology is never used as a dose.
- **kept**: products outside the pack kept as lines to add as typed.
- **noise**: products read right that say their line names more (a warning she looks at for nothing).
- **must-warn**: what the reading made of each case: read whole, read in part with the warning, kept as typed, or failed (silent, a false product, lost).
- The time of a reading per page (not a bar: machines differ).

## Bars

- No false product on printed pages, with or without the warning, for the engine the app ships and its paths (`paddleocr`, `pdf-text-layer`, `pdf-scanned-paddleocr`) and for the exact text: `maxFalsePrint` is 0 for them.
- On handwritten pages (the proxy), no more false products without the warning than the baseline (`maxSilentHand`, 0 for the engine the app ships): a combination read in part that says what else its line names is not counted there, since it is never pre-ticked and its line is offered as typed.
- The Tesseract sources may not do worse than their baseline: they are not shipped, they measure the reading on noisier text.
- No recall below the baseline, on printed and on handwritten pages, for every source.
- Every must-warn case read whole or with its warning.

A baseline is changed only in a reviewed pull request that says why. When the reading improves, raise the bars to the new figures.

With the resolver of pull request 59 (phonetic folding) merged on a local branch on 2026-11-06, every bar holds: PaddleOCR keeps 244 / 244 on printed pages and 128 / 136 on the proxy with no false product, the exact text and the PDF paths are unchanged, Tesseract single block finds 68 / 136 on the proxy with 13 false products of which 7 warned (the new one, pyrimethamine read in "Pyniméllamine", says the line also names sulfadoxine), and Tesseract automatic layout 42 / 92 with 4 false products. Two rules of the reading came from that run: a posology word is known as written and as it sounds ("price" is "prise", or MONOCRIXO was proposed for "1 ap en price unique"), and the slips the snap allows go by the length of the word as read.

## Known limits

- The combinations Tesseract read in part without a warning: "Fer + Acide folique" whose folic acid became unreadable ("ride pique", "qd rtgue", "acide otique"), "Tardyféron B9" read "B7" or "B?" (TARDYFERON alone: the line names no substance), and one SP whose sulfadoxine became "ubfaderre". A line that names nothing more cannot say it. PaddleOCR read none of these in part.
- "Fe" for iron is not read as a substance name.
- The set is synthetic, its photos simulated, and its handwriting only a font: real prescriptions will read worse (spec, section 4.8).
